Research

Questions that travel from discovery to application.

The work focuses on mitochondrial function, how it can be measured, and what limits responsible translation across biological settings.

Comparative and extreme mitochondrial biology

How do animals sustain mitochondrial function under oxygen limitation, acidosis, thermal pressure, or exceptional energetic demand? Comparative physiology provides mechanisms to test, rather than direct claims of translation.

Explore this research area →

Organ preservation and critical care

Research investigates mitochondrial metabolism, redox biology, electrophysiology, and meaningful measurements of injury and recovery during ex vivo organ preservation and machine perfusion.

Explore this research area →

Measurement, monitoring, and biosensing

Work explores how respiratory behaviour, redox biology, and spectral or optical signals can become robust research measurements in demanding workflows.

Explore this research area →

Environmental resilience and the blue economy

Mitochondrial and cardiac physiology are applied to questions of thermal stress, performance, welfare, and resilience in aquaculture species.
