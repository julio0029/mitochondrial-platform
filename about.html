About the platform

Mitochondria are where mechanism meets consequence.

Jules Devaux works across comparative physiology, mitochondrial bioenergetics, experimental design, and translational research.

A research platform, not a promise machine.

The platform connects fundamental biology with questions in health, organ preservation, environmental resilience, and measurement technology. Its starting point is a precise biological question and its endpoint is a credible next experiment, measurement, or validation decision.

How the work is approached

Comparative systems reveal mechanisms that can sharpen hypotheses. They do not, on their own, establish a clinical, commercial, or environmental solution. Each proposed application must be tested in the relevant setting and earn its claim through appropriate evidence, partnership, ethics, and regulatory pathways.
