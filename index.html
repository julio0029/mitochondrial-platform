01 — The platform

Nature has already solved some of biology’s hardest energy problems.

This platform investigates how animals tolerate hypoxia, thermal stress, acidosis, and extraordinary energetic demand—and asks what those mechanisms can responsibly contribute to health, conservation, and industry.

Its purpose is to bring together established and emerging research streams into a connected mitochondrial research capability. The ambition is rigorous: understand the biology first, test translation carefully, and communicate the difference between published evidence, active research, and early-stage concepts.

02 — Research & translation

A connected portfolio

Foundational research

Extreme mitochondrial biology

Research across hypoxia-tolerant fish and sharks, high-performance insect flight muscle, and other comparative systems examines how mitochondrial function is maintained under environmental and metabolic stress.

 Clinical translation

Organ preservation & mitochondrial injury

A commitment to evidence

No shortcuts from a good idea to a health claim.

The platform distinguishes its peer-reviewed findings from active studies and exploratory opportunities. Any clinical, conservation, or commercial application must earn its place through appropriate validation, partnership, ethics, and regulatory pathways.
