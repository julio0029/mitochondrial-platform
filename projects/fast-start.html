Marsden Fast Start · Fundamental science

Powering the flight of the bumblebee.

A Marsden Fund Fast Start research programme asks how bumblebee flight muscle maintains extraordinary mitochondrial energy delivery during sustained flight.

The question

Bumblebee flight muscle supports one of biology’s highest sustained metabolic rates. The project uses this exceptional system to investigate how mitochondrial architecture, energy transfer, and oxidative stress management are coordinated.

Why it is useful

Fundamental comparative work creates sharper, testable hypotheses about energetic resilience. It does not presume a direct application, but it can reveal mechanisms worth testing in other biological and technological settings.

Status

Independent research programme supported through the Marsden Fund Fast Start scheme.
