MitoSafe

MitoSafe is an early-stage, nature-informed research concept for preserving functional mitochondria.

Scientific basis

The concept draws on organisms that survive freezing or desiccation. Their protective biology suggests questions that may be useful for investigating mitochondrial preservation, membrane integrity, and respiratory recovery.

What is not claimed

Long-term preservation of functional isolated mitochondria remains a major technical challenge. MitoSafe is not a validated product, therapy, or biobanking service; its possible applications require rigorous feasibility and performance testing.

Status

Early-stage concept under development.
