02 — Clinical translation

Organ preservation & mitochondrial injury

This stream investigates what happens to mitochondrial function during organ preservation, machine perfusion, and ischaemia–reperfusion.

Focus

It examines mitochondrial metabolism, redox biology, and electrophysiology during ex-vivo organ preservation, helping define how injury and recovery can be measured more meaningfully in transplant settings.

Why this matters

Better evidence about mitochondrial injury could help refine research approaches to organ assessment. Any clinical adoption, however, requires independent validation, ethics approval, regulatory assessment, and appropriate clinical partnerships.

Status

Active translational research. It is not presented here as a clinical service or a claim of improved patient outcomes.
