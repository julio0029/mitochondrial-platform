03 — Diagnostics

ROS & metabolic monitoring

Technology and assay development seeks to make dynamic mitochondrial biology more measurable across experimental, environmental, and future translational settings.

Focus

Work explores reactive oxygen species, cytochrome-linked spectral signals, respiratory behaviour, and stress signatures. The aim is to improve temporal resolution and practical measurement of mitochondrial state.

Translation boundary

The intended outputs are robust research measurements and validation-ready monitoring concepts—not a declared clinical diagnostic. Each measurement must be technically validated in its intended context.

Status

Research and development.
