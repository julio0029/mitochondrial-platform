Extreme mitochondrial biology

Comparative physiology is used to understand how mitochondria maintain function under oxygen limitation, acidosis, thermal pressure, and exceptional energetic demand.

The research question

Why can some animals sustain mitochondrial performance in conditions that cause injury or failure in others? Work includes hypoxia-tolerant triplefin fish and sharks, alongside high-performance Hymenoptera flight muscle.

How it is studied

Approaches include high-resolution respirometry, isolated mitochondrial preparations, redox assays, membrane-potential measurements, and cardiac physiology models. The platform treats comparative biology as a source of mechanisms to test—not as proof that a solution will translate directly to humans.

Status

Published work and ongoing research. See the University of Auckland profile for publications and current affiliations.
