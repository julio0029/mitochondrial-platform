05 — Blue economy

Aquaculture resilience

This collaborative stream applies mitochondrial physiology to questions of cardiac performance and stress resilience in aquaculture species.

Focus

Environmental pressures such as temperature change can alter energetic capacity and cardiovascular function. The work links those pressures with mechanistic measurements of mitochondrial and cardiac physiology.

Translation boundary

Any sector application needs to be tested in the relevant species, production conditions, and decision context. The platform therefore treats this as an evidence-building research stream rather than a ready-made sector solution.

Status

Collaborative research stream.
